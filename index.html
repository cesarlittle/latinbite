Our Story

From Venezuela to Iceland

We are a Venezuelan family that came to Iceland with a dream: to share the flavors of our homeland with the world.

It all started in 2022, when we decided to bring to Iceland one of Venezuela's most beloved snacks: the tequeño. With our grandmother's recipe and a passion for good food, Tequevik was born.

Today, from our small factory in Reykjavik, we craft each tequeño with the same love and dedication we learned at home. We use the finest Icelandic cheese and artisanal techniques that guarantee that crispy outside and creamy inside that defines us.
